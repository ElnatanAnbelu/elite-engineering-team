## What the pipeline encodes
- **One orchestrator, five gates.** The [[staff-engineer|Staff Engineer]] is the only spawner and the only gatekeeper. Every gate is the same three-question check (production-grade? anything skipped? proud to ship?) defined in [[DOCTRINE]].
- **Questions live in Stage 1 only.** [[pm|PM]] runs intake; the four other Leadership roles add their lenses; the output is one Leadership Brief that powers Stages 2–5 with zero further questions.
- **Stages are sequential; specialists within a stage run in parallel** — each chevron's plates are the roles working that stage at once.
- **A failed gate** returns a targeted correction (file → defect → fix) to the responsible stage, then re-gates.
